# Residential roofing services

JR&CO installs, replaces and repairs roofs on homes. Services:

- **Architectural asphalt shingles.** Thicker, layered shingles with a dimensional look. The most common choice for homes; good value and a wide color range.
- **Standing seam metal.** Metal panels joined by raised seams. Long-lasting, sheds snow and rain well, and suits modern or farmhouse styles.
- **Clay or concrete tile.** Heavy, very durable tile roofing, common on Spanish, Mediterranean and Southwestern homes. The roof structure must be able to carry the weight; the inspector checks this.
- **Synthetic composite.** Engineered shingles that look like slate or cedar shake but weigh less and need less upkeep.
- **Roof repairs.** Leaks, missing or damaged shingles, flashing, and storm or hail damage.
- **Gutters.** New gutters, replacement and repair.
- **Siding.** Siding installation and replacement.
- **Solar integration.** Roofing work planned around solar panels, so the roof and panels work together.

What Ella should not do: recommend a specific material for a caller's home, or say how long a job takes. The free inspection is where the inspector recommends materials. Offer the inspection instead.
