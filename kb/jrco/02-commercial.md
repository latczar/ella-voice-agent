# Commercial roofing services

JR&CO's Commercial Division handles flat and low-slope roofs on business, industrial and multi-unit buildings. Systems:

- **TPO** (thermoplastic polyolefin). A white, single-ply membrane with heat-welded seams. Reflects heat, which helps keep cooling costs down.
- **EPDM.** A durable synthetic rubber membrane, usually black. A long-standing, proven flat-roof system.
- **PVC.** A single-ply membrane with heat-welded seams. Strong resistance to chemicals, grease and fire, so it's common on restaurants and industrial buildings.
- **Modified bitumen** ("mod-bit"). Asphalt-based sheets in layers. Tough and good for roofs with foot traffic.
- **Fluid-applied coatings.** A liquid coating rolled or sprayed over an existing roof to seal and extend its life, often without a full tear-off.
- **Preventive maintenance programs.** Scheduled inspections and minor repairs to catch problems early and extend roof life.

For commercial callers, useful details are: company name, type of facility (for example warehouse, retail, office, multi-family), rough roof size or number of buildings, and the property address.
